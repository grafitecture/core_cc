Consulting with substance. Coaching with impact.

Practice and business development. Collaboration and communication.

In practices and businesses, people with different roles, experiences and expectations come together every day. They work in teams, talk to patients, customers and external partners, and make decisions. How well this works depends largely on how people communicate, understand one another and shape the way they work together.

Personal experiences, expectations and habitual behaviour play a part here, just as organisational structures, processes and responsibilities do. All of these factors influence how people perceive situations, make decisions and deal with one another in their working lives.

This is where my consulting comes in. I support owners and teams of medical and dental practices as well as small and medium-sized businesses in developing their collaboration and operational processes. In open dialogue, we look at different perspectives, recognise connections and work out opportunities for improvement. Much of this begins with understanding oneself and others. Those who can better make sense of their own reactions and understand how others see things gain orientation and confidence in dealing with one another. On this basis, we jointly develop concepts that fit the specific requirements of the practice or business, taking into account both the needs of the people involved and the organisation’s goals and circumstances. I accompany the implementation step by step, so that new ways of communicating, collaborating and organising can develop and take hold in everyday working life.

My work is shaped by more than 25 years of professional experience in the dental industry and medical technology, during which I was responsible, among other things, for business incubation of innovative medical development projects. Close collaboration with a range of disciplines across medicine, technology and business, my academic background in business administration, educational science and psychology, and my many years as a self-employed business coach form the basis of my consulting approach today.
