3. Hosting by comvos

This website is operated on a managed server of comvos online medien GmbH, C4, 18, 68159 Mannheim, Germany. comvos processes the data exclusively on our behalf and in accordance with our instructions. We have concluded a data processing agreement with comvos pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR.

The server is located in a data centre of Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, in Germany. Hetzner acts as a sub-processor for comvos. No data are transferred to countries outside the European Union.

Server log files

Each time the website is accessed, the server automatically stores:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • date and time of access
  • page or file accessed
  • status code and amount of data transferred
  • previously visited page (referrer), if your browser sends it
  • browser type and version, and operating system

We need these data to deliver the website, to operate it securely and reliably and to find errors. The legal basis is our legitimate interest pursuant to Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The data are not combined with other data sources and are not analysed for advertising or analytics purposes.

The log files are stored only for as long as necessary for these purposes and are then deleted automatically, unless they are needed to investigate a security incident.

Encryption

The connection to this website is encrypted using TLS. You can recognise this by “https://” in the address bar.

4. Storage in your browser

We use no cookies. Your browser merely stores files such as the font, images and stylesheet in its cache so that the site loads faster on your next visit. This is strictly necessary to provide the website (Section 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG, the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act) and is not used to recognise visitors. You can clear the cache at any time in your browser settings.

5. Contact by email or phone

If you contact us by email or phone, we process the details you provide (such as your name, email address, phone number and the content of your enquiry) in order to answer your enquiry and prepare a conversation. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your enquiry is aimed at a contract, otherwise our legitimate interest in answering it pursuant to Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. We delete the data once the enquiry has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

7. Links to other websites

This website contains links to other websites, such as Kim Burzan’s LinkedIn profile or the Grafitecture website. These are simple links: only when you click a link do you go to the other website, and its operator receives data such as your IP address. The respective operator is responsible for processing there, and their privacy policy applies.

8. No disclosure, no automated decisions

Apart from the service providers named here, we do not pass your data on to third parties unless we are legally obliged to do so. No automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR, including profiling, takes place. You are not obliged to provide us with personal data; however, the website cannot be delivered without your IP address being transmitted.

9. Your rights

You have the following rights:

  • access to the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16),
  • erasure (Art. 17),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18) and
  • data portability (Art. 20).

To exercise these rights, write to us at info@core-cc.com.

Right to object

Where we process your data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR), you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). We will then no longer process your data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Right to lodge a complaint

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Der Hessische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit), Gustav-Stresemann-Ring 1, 65189 Wiesbaden, Germany (datenschutz.hessen.de).
