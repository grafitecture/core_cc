3. Server log files

Each time you access this website, your browser automatically sends data to the server, where the following are stored in log files:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • date and time of access
  • page or file requested
  • status code and amount of data transferred
  • previously visited page (referrer), if your browser sends it
  • browser type and version, and operating system

We need these data to deliver the website, to ensure its security and stability (for example to fend off attacks) and to analyse errors. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in providing the website securely and reliably. The data are not combined with other data sources and are not analysed for advertising or analytics purposes.

Log files are stored only for as long as necessary for these purposes and are then deleted automatically. If a specific security incident has to be investigated, we keep the relevant entries until it has been resolved.

4. Encryption

This website uses SSL/TLS encryption. You can recognise an encrypted connection by “https://” in your browser’s address bar. Data you transmit to us cannot be read by third parties.

5. No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising services. The font and images are stored on our own server; no content is loaded from third parties such as Google Fonts, map services or social media plug-ins.

Your browser stores files such as the font, images and stylesheet in its cache so that the site loads faster on your next visit. This is technically necessary to provide the website (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act) and is not used to recognise visitors.

6. Contact by email or phone

If you contact us by email or phone, we process the details you provide (such as your name, email address, phone number and the content of your enquiry) in order to deal with your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry relates to a contract or steps prior to entering into one, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – our legitimate interest lies in answering your enquiry.

We delete your details once your enquiry has been fully dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations (for example under commercial or tax law) require us to keep them.

7. Website support

This website is designed, built and maintained by Grafitecture GmbH, Rebenstr. 9a, 68309 Mannheim, Germany (www.grafitecture.de). Grafitecture maintains the content and deploys updates to the server. In doing so, Grafitecture does not access visitors’ data. Should this be necessary in an individual case, for example to fix an error, it is done solely on our behalf and on our instructions (Art. 28 GDPR).

8. Links to other websites

This website contains links to other websites, such as Kim Burzan’s LinkedIn profile or the Grafitecture website. These are simple links: only when you click a link do you go to the other website, and its operator receives data such as your IP address. The respective operator is responsible for processing there, and their privacy policy applies.

9. Disclosure and further information

Apart from the service providers named in this policy, we do not pass your data on to third parties unless we are legally obliged to do so. You are not obliged to provide us with personal data; however, the website cannot be delivered without your IP address being transmitted. No automated decision-making, including profiling, takes place.

10. Your rights

You have the following rights at any time:

  • access to the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR) and
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR).

An informal message to info@core-cc.com is sufficient.

11. Right to object

Where we process your data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). We will then no longer process your data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
